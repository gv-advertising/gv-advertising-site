Why it stays clean

Advertising is not referring.

01

California Business & Professions Code §650(g): paying for advertising through a third party that does not recommend, endorse or select a licensee is not a patient referral.

02

No per-patient fees, no per-lead fees, no commissions — a flat monthly fee, written into the agreement (AB 1280, federal Anti-Kickback Statute).

03

Hospice campaigns run only inside your approved service area, with a full CDPH license.
